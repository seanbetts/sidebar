## What is sideBar?

sideBar is an AI assistant for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and web. It gives you a
single place to take notes, manage tasks, save websites, and work with files,
all alongside an AI that can read your content, help you think, and get things
done.

## What makes sideBar different from ChatGPT or Claude.ai?

Most AI assistants start fresh with every conversation and know nothing about
you. sideBar builds a persistent picture of you from your notes, tasks, saved
websites, files, and memories, and brings that context into every conversation
automatically. You also bring your own API key and choose your provider, so you
are not locked into one company or model. The Skills Store lets you extend
sideBar with capabilities that can take actions, connect to external services,
and go well beyond simple question and answer.

## What can I use sideBar for?

Practically anything you would ask a knowledgeable assistant. Common examples
include drafting and editing writing, researching topics and summarising
articles, planning your day and managing tasks, brainstorming ideas, organising
notes across projects, saving web pages for later reference, getting quick
answers from your own files and documents, and automating tasks through skills
like web search, email, or audio transcription. Because sideBar knows your
content, it can connect the dots across your notes, tasks, websites, and files
in ways a generic chatbot cannot.

## I just signed up. What should I do first?

Four things will get you the most out of sideBar quickly:

1. **Fill out your profile** in Settings. Your name, job, and home location help
   sideBar tailor its responses from the start.
2. **Start a conversation** in Chat. Ask sideBar anything to see how it works.
3. **Save a website or upload a file**, then ask sideBar about it. This is where
   the context advantage becomes obvious.
4. **Browse the Skills Store** and install anything useful to you. Skills extend
   what sideBar can do, from web search to email to transcription.

## What platforms does sideBar run on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac, and in your browser. Your content syncs across all of them
in real time.

## Do I need to bring my own API key?

Yes. sideBar works with Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. You choose the provider
and add your own API key in Settings. Your key is encrypted before being stored
and only the last four characters are ever shown back to you.

## How does pricing work?

There is no subscription fee for sideBar itself. You connect your own API key
from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google and pay that provider directly for the AI
usage you generate. Costs depend on which provider you choose and how much you
use the app.

## Which AI provider should I use?

All three supported providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) work with sideBar's
core features. The best choice depends on your existing accounts and
preferences.

## Will I lose my memories and personalisation if I switch provider?

No. Your memories, notes, tasks, saved websites, files, and all other content
are stored in sideBar, not with your AI provider. Switching from one provider
to another has no effect on any of that. You can swap freely at any time from
Settings and everything carries over.

## What is the Skills Store?

The Skills Store is a library of optional capabilities you can add to sideBar.
Skills can search the web, transcribe audio and video, send emails, connect to
third-party services, and more. Once installed, sideBar uses them automatically
during conversations when they are relevant. See
[Skills Store](/manual/skills-store) for the full list and setup instructions.

## How does sync work across devices?

Your content syncs across every device where you are signed in. When the device
that made the change is online, updates usually appear on your other devices
within seconds. If you make changes while offline, they stay local to that
device until it reconnects, then sync automatically. You can see queued changes
in Settings under the User tab.

## Does sideBar work offline?

Cached notes, tasks, websites, files, and chat history work offline. New notes,
tasks, website saves, and file imports queue locally and sync when the device
reconnects. Sending chat messages still requires a connection.

If something is missing on the current device because it was never cached there,
sideBar shows that directly.

## What does sideBar store?

sideBar stores your notes, tasks, saved websites, files, chat conversations,
memories, and settings. Everything syncs across your devices via your account.
See our [Privacy Policy](https://trysidebar.ai/privacy) for full details.

## What file types does sideBar support?

sideBar accepts a wide range of formats: PDFs, Word documents, plain text,
images (JPG, PNG, and others), audio files, video files, spreadsheets (Excel
and CSV), and presentations (PowerPoint and similar). See
[Files](/manual/files) for the full breakdown.

## Does sideBar have its own personality?

Yes. sideBar has an identity (a name, a tone of voice, a set of values, and
boundaries it holds) and a soul (a sense of purpose, operating principles, and
an understanding of how it should grow with you over time). These shape every
response and make sideBar feel consistent rather than generic. You can view them
in Settings under the sideBar tab. See [Personality](/manual/personality) for
the full explanation.

## Can I control how sideBar communicates with me?

Partly. sideBar manages its own identity and soul, but there are two settings
you control directly in Settings under the User tab: communication style (how
formal or casual sideBar is with you) and working relationship (whether sideBar
acts as a partner, a coach, a teacher, or something else). These work alongside
sideBar's personality rather than overriding it.

## Does sideBar change over time?

Yes. As sideBar learns more about you through conversations and the content you
create, it updates its soul to stay aligned with who you are and what you need.
Each update is versioned and timestamped. You can ask sideBar to update its
identity and soul at any time, but cannot edit them directly; they belong to
sideBar.

## Is sideBar accessible?

sideBar follows standard iOS and macOS accessibility conventions. The app works
with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type, and you can adjust the text size in Settings
under the App tab (System, Small, Medium, or Large). Biometric lock (Face ID
or Touch ID) is available if your device supports it.

## How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Just tell sideBar in Chat, for example "I found a bug with X" or "I'd love a
feature that does Y". sideBar will file a report automatically and send you a
link to the issue. See [Getting Help](/manual/getting-help) for more.
