## What is sideBar?

sideBar is an AI assistant for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and web. It gives you a
single place to take notes, manage tasks, save websites, and work with files,
all alongside an AI that can read your content, help you think, and get things
done.

## What makes sideBar different from ChatGPT or Claude.ai?

Most AI assistants start fresh with every conversation and know nothing about
you. sideBar builds a persistent picture of you from your notes, tasks, saved
websites, files, and memories, and brings that context into every conversation
automatically. You also bring your own API key and choose your provider, so you
are not locked into one company or model. The Skills Store lets you extend
sideBar with capabilities that can take actions, connect to external services,
and go well beyond simple question and answer.

## What platforms does sideBar run on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac, and in your browser. Your content syncs across all of them
in real time.

## What is the Skills Store?

The Skills Store is a library of optional capabilities you can add to sideBar.
Skills can search the web, transcribe audio and video, send emails, connect to
third-party services, and more. Once installed, sideBar uses them automatically
during conversations when they are relevant. See
[Skills Store](/manual/skills-store) for the full list and setup instructions.

## Do I need to bring my own API key?

Yes. sideBar works with Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. You choose the provider
and add your own API key in Settings. Your key is encrypted before being stored
and only the last four characters are ever shown back to you.

## How does pricing work?

There is no subscription fee for sideBar itself. You connect your own API key
from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google and pay that provider directly for the AI
usage you generate. Costs depend on which provider you choose and how much you
use the app.

## Which AI provider should I use?

All three supported providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) work with sideBar's
core features. The best choice depends on your existing accounts and
preferences.

## Does sideBar work offline?

Reading your existing notes, tasks, websites, and files works offline. Sending
chat messages, saving new content, or syncing changes requires a connection.
If something isn't saving, check your connectivity first.

## What does sideBar store?

sideBar stores your notes, tasks, saved websites, files, chat conversations,
memories, and settings. Everything syncs across your devices via your account.
See our [Privacy Policy](https://trysidebar.ai/privacy) for full details.

## What file types does sideBar support?

sideBar accepts a wide range of formats: PDFs, Word documents, plain text,
images (JPG, PNG, and others), audio files, video files, spreadsheets (Excel
and CSV), and presentations (PowerPoint and similar). See
[Files](/manual/files) for the full breakdown.

## How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Just tell sideBar in Chat, for example "I found a bug with X" or "I'd love a
feature that does Y". sideBar will file a report automatically and send you a
link to the issue. See [Getting Help](/manual/getting-help) for more.
