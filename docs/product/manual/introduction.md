sideBar is an AI assistant available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and in your
web browser. It gives you a single place to take notes, manage tasks,
browse websites, and work with files — all with an AI assistant that can help
you get things done.

The core idea is that your content and sideBar live together. When you
ask the sideBar a question, it can pull from your notes, check your tasks, or
browse the web on your behalf, without you having to switch between apps or copy
things around.

## What's in the App

- **Chat** — your main interface with sideBar. Ask questions, give
  instructions, or just think out loud.
- **Notes** — a full-featured editor for capturing and organizing your writing.
- **Tasks** — a lightweight to-do system sideBar can read and update.
- **Websites** — saved pages and browsing context sideBar can reference.
- **Files** — documents and attachments you want sideBar to work with.

## Skills

Skills extend what sideBar can do. There are three kinds:

- **Required skills** are always active and power sideBar's identity, soul and
  allow users to submit product feedback and bug reports.
- **Standard skills** come pre-installed and cover common capabilities like
  allowing sideBar to work with your notes, tasks, and files as well as create
  memories and adjust settings. You can turn each of them on or off.
- **Store skills** are additional capabilities you can install yourself.
  Think of it like upgrading sideBar's capabilities. Examples include
  transcribing YouTube videos and integrations with services like Gmail and
  Spotify — some store skills require an API key from that service to set up.
