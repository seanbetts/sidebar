If something isn't working the way you expect, here are the best places to start.

## Ask the Assistant

sideBar can answer questions about sideBar itself. If you're not sure how a feature works or where to find something, just ask in [Chat](/manual/chat) — "how do I add a memory?" or "what can Skills do?" are perfectly good questions.

## Check Settings → Skills

A lot of functionality in sideBar is powered by [Skills](/manual/skills). If a feature seems to be missing, it may just be that the relevant Skill isn't enabled. Settings → Skills is a quick first thing to check.

## Paid Features

Some features require a paid plan. sideBar will let you know when you try to use something that isn't included in your current plan — no guessing required.

## The FAQ

The [FAQ](/faq/general) section of these docs covers common questions and known gotchas. If something is tricky or frequently misunderstood, it's probably in there.

## Contact Support

If you've checked the above and still need help, reach out via the in-app support option in Settings. Include a description of what you were trying to do and what happened instead — that makes it much faster to help you.

## Offline

Reading your existing content always works offline. Creating new notes, sending messages, or adding tasks requires a connection. If something isn't saving, check your connectivity first.
