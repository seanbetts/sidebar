## Account

Your profile stores the details sideBar can reference when it's useful to know who you are. You can edit your name, job title, employer, location, pronouns, and date of birth. Tap your profile photo to replace it with a new one from your camera or photo library.

When you're ready to sign out, you'll find that option at the bottom of the Account section.

## Assistant Personalisation

This is where you shape how sideBar works with you. All fields save
automatically a moment after you stop typing — there's no save button to tap.

**Communication style** controls how sideBar speaks — from casual and
conversational to formal and precise.

**Working relationship** sets the dynamic — whether sideBar acts as a
collaborative partner, an active coach, a patient teacher, or something else.

The **Advanced** section exposes the full identity and soul configuration,
including sideBar's name, tone, values, purpose, and operating principles. See
the [Personality](/manual/personality) page for a full explanation of how these
work.

## Memories

Memories are reference documents that sideBar draws on during every conversation. Think of them as persistent context: background about your work, preferences you want it to respect, or details about ongoing projects.

You can add and edit memories directly from Settings, and search across them when your list grows. sideBar reads relevant memories automatically — you don't need to mention them.

## AI Provider

Choose which AI provider powers sideBar: Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google. Once you've selected a provider, add your API key for that service. After saving, the last four characters of the key are displayed as confirmation — the full key is never shown again.

## Skills

Manage which Standard and Store skills are active. See the [Skills](/manual/skills) page for a full explanation of what skills are and how they work.

## App Settings

**Text size** — choose Small, Normal, Large, or Extra Large, or let sideBar follow your device's system accessibility setting.

**Biometric lock** — require Face ID or Touch ID each time you open sideBar. This option only appears if your device supports biometric authentication.

**Temperature units** — set whether weather shown to sideBar is in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

## Sync Status

If you made changes while offline, they queue up and sync automatically once your connection returns. You can check this section to see how many writes are pending.
