## Purpose

Define the product voice, terminology, and UI writing rules for sideBar.

## Why this matters

Words are part of the product experience. The design system needs one place that defines tone, naming, and content standards across UI, docs, support, and marketing.

## Scope

In scope:

- Product voice
- UI labels and terminology
- Empty states, errors, and success copy
- The boundary between product voice and assistant identity

Out of scope:

- Model prompts
- Runtime assistant identity authoring

## Open questions

- What should sideBar's product voice sound like in a few words?
- Which terms are canonical across app and docs?
- Where should the product feel warm or opinionated, and where should it stay neutral?
